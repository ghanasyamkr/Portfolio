Overview

Building a scalable solitaire for live content updates.

The goal was to launch a feature-complete solitaire that could scale events, economy, and content drops without tech debt slowing the team down.

Goals

  • Ship a polished solitaire loop with reliable undo and modular mechanics.
  • Design a LiveOps-ready architecture for fast experiments and events.
  • Reduce draw calls and loading overhead across UI-heavy flows.

My role

  • Owned gameplay systems, architecture patterns, and feature integrations.
  • Built tooling and asset pipeline improvements for content speed.
  • Partnered with design to tune retention, economy, and progression.

Gameplay systems

Modular mechanics with reliable undo.

Systems were built with Factory and Command patterns to enable fast feature additions and stable undo flows.

Command-driven moves

Every player action is encapsulated, making undo and analytics consistent and safe.

Factory-based rules

Game rules and table setups are data-driven for rapid event reskins and variations.

State + persistence

Session recovery and save state handle long play sessions and offline continuity.

Retention + LiveOps

Loops designed for daily return.

Progression, economy, and event hooks were built to support rapid iteration and cohort testing.
